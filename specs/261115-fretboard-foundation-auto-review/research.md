# Research: Fretboard Foundation Auto Review Fixes

**Created:** 2026-11-15 · **Source:** [PRD](fretboard-foundation-auto-review-PRD.md)

## Research Questions and Answers

1. Stepper order: box-number order starting at box 1, as AC-SC-3.1 reads (PRD open question 1).
2. Triads with an open string at fret 12 or more: list only under the inversion they truly are (question 2).
3. Unpaced tuner device: show a notice, no automatic fallback (question 3).
4. `'unsafe-inline'` for styles: remove if feasible, else document (question 4); findings in [implementation-notes.md](implementation-notes.md).
